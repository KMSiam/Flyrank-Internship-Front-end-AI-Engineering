1. 3D Scene Architecture & Lighting Rig

The experience models a central "Quantum Neural Node" core surrounded by an orbiting tensor flux ring:

1. Procedural Geometries (0KB Model Overhead)
  • Quantum Node (Icosahedron): High-surface faceted node geometry (1.4 radius, detail level 1).
  • Tensor Flux (Torus Knot): Intricate mathematical knot (0.9 radius, tube 0.32, 100 tubular segments).
  • Neural Matrix (Dodecahedron): Platonic solid with 12 regular pentagonal faces.
  • Orbiting Tensor Ring: Surrounding wireframe particle ring rotating across contrasting axis.
2. Three-Point Physical Lighting Rig
  • Ambient Light: 1.2 intensity baseline illumination ensuring zero black crush.
  • Key Light: Directional light at (5, 8, 5), 2.5 intensity casting crisp specular highlights.
  • Fill Light: Cyan point light at (-5, -2, -3), 3.0 intensity providing deep space contrast.
  • Rim Light: Emerald point light at (3, -4, 2), 2.5 intensity accentuating geometry contours.

3. Responsible Shipping & Performance Budget (FE-10 Lens)

1. Strict Size Budget

By generating geometries procedurally rather than downloading external uncompressed GLB/OBJ files (often 5–20MB), model transfer size is 0 KB. Total Three.js route chunk is only 3.27 KB.

2. Mobile Thermal Safety

renderer.setPixelRatio(Math.min(window.devicePixelRatio, 2)) prevents modern 3x and 4x retina smartphone displays from rendering billions of unnecessary fragments, preserving battery and thermal ceilings.

3. Lazy-Load & Fallback

Mounted via next/dynamic with ssr: false and an animated skeleton fallback. Honors prefers-reduced-motion by auto-pausing scene rotation.
