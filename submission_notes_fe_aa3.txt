3. UNIFORMS UTILIZED:
- u_time (float): Continuous delta elapsed time driving 4-octave Fractional Brownian Motion (fBm) fluid waves.
- u_resolution (vec2): Dynamic viewport dimensions used for aspect-ratio correction, preventing stretching on mobile and ultrawide screens.
- u_mouse (vec2): Pointer coordinates creating an interactive liquid wake with exponential spatial falloff.

4. RESPONSIBLE SHIPPING & REDUCED-MOTION FALLBACK:
- devicePixelRatio Capping: Capped at Math.min(window.devicePixelRatio || 1, 2) to eliminate thermal runaway and battery drain on mobile displays.
- Tab Inactivity: Listens to document "visibilitychange" to freeze the render loop when minimized or in background tabs.
- prefers-reduced-motion: Users with reduced motion preferences automatically receive a static, accessible brand aurora gradient without allocating WebGL resources.
- Manual Play/Pause Control: Interactive button in the hero header allows users to pause and resume the animation at any time.
