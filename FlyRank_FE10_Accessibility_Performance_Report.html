3. WCAG 2.1 AA & AI-Specific Accessibility Remediation

1. Polite Streaming Announcements

Wrapped the chat message list in role="region" with aria-live="polite" and aria-atomic="false". Prevents screen readers from getting flooded by rapid 30ms token updates while announcing completion cleanly.

2. Keyboard-Reachable Stop Control

The Stop button replaces the Send button in the exact same DOM focus index, equipped with aria-label="Stop AI streaming generation" and visible focus-visible:ring-2 outline.
